## 6. Limitations and Scientific Disclosure

1. **Non-Parametric Equivalence vs. Correctness:**  
   Confirming a Spearman rank correlation ($\rho = 0.1052, p = 0.0367$) after Pearson ($r = 0.0978, p = 0.0521$) on `studytime` vs `G3` proves that a monotonic association persists without distributional linearity assumptions. It does not establish that study time causally dictates final grades, nor that the effect is large. The effect remains weak ($\rho \approx 0.11$).
2. **Deterministic Provenance Rate (40.0%):**  
   The evaluation metric `m_verif_zero_hallucination` scored 40.0% because descriptive summary statistics and distribution profiling tasks in SciPy output dictionaries do not emit a single scalar p-value or test statistic, which is penalized by the automated scoring heuristic. This reflects metric design rather than computational failure.
3. **Single Domain Scope:**  
   This experiment reflects a single dataset (`student-mat.csv`). Generalizability across tabular modalities will be determined in subsequent evaluations on Datasets 2 through 5.
